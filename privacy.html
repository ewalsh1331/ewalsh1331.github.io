Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

kitchen-dashboard is a personal app used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, the app reads, read-only, the owner's Google Calendar events and the owner's own Google Sheets.

How data is used

The data is used only to show the owner's schedule and personal information on a display in the owner's home. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Google sign-in credentials and any cached data are stored only on the owner's device. No data is sent to any server other than Google's.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
